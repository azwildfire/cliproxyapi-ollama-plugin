Add an account

Works like ollama signin: the proxy creates a device key, you approve it once on ollama.com, and requests are signed with it from then on. No key leaves the proxy.

Open the link, sign in to ollama.com, and approve the device. This page keeps checking until the account is connected.

ollama.com/connect

Create a key at ollama.com/settings/keys and paste it here. It is validated against your account and stored in the proxy's auth directory like any other credential.

Reuse the key of a machine that already ran ollama signin: paste the contents of ~/.ollama/id_ed25519 (inside the official Ollama container: /root/.ollama/id_ed25519).

The same flows are available from the Auth Files page (Ollama Cloud login card) and from the API: /v8/management/oauth/auth-url?provider=ollama-cloud.
